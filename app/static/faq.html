Questions and answers

Can't find your answer? Ask Kiln, our support assistant, in the corner of the page, or write to support@pixelkiln.example.

What is a credit?

A credit pays for one standard image. Upscaling an image costs 2 credits. Credits renew on your billing date each month and do not carry over.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, if you ask within 7 days of the payment and have not used any credits from it. In all other cases payments are not refunded. Send refund requests to billing@pixelkiln.example.

How do I cancel or change my plan?

Go to Settings > Billing. After cancelling, your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

Are there discount codes?

No. The only discount is annual billing: Pro costs $120 per year and Studio $390 per seat per year.

Can I use the images commercially?

On Pro and Studio, yes. Images made on the Free plan are for personal use only.

What happens to my prompts and images?

We keep them for 30 days so you can see your history. You can delete them earlier in Settings > Privacy.

What can't I generate?

Sexual content involving minors, sexual images of real people, realistic images of real people without their consent, content that promotes violence or hatred, and images meant to deceive, such as fake documents.

How can I pay?

With major credit and debit cards.
