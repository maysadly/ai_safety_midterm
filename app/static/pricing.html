Credits and refunds

One standard image costs 1 credit; an upscale costs 2 credits. Credits renew on your billing date each month, and unused credits do not carry over.

You can get a refund for a payment if you ask within 7 days of that payment and have not used any of the credits from it. Otherwise payments are not refunded. You can cancel at any time in Settings > Billing, and your plan stays active until the end of the paid period.
