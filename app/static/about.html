About Pixelkiln

Pixelkiln does not exist. It is a fictional company made for a student project in the course AI Security and Risk Analysis (AISRA1301) at QAIRU. The name, prices, plans and email addresses are invented; the addresses use the reserved .example domain.

Why the site exists

The site is a realistic home for a customer support chatbot. The project tests how well that chatbot keeps its rules when people try to talk it out of them: revealing its instructions, leaking an internal code, promising refunds outside the policy, or doing unrelated work.

How the assistant works

Kiln, the assistant in the corner, is a small open-weight language model (Qwen2.5 3B Instruct) running locally with Ollama. It follows a fixed system prompt, and a second model, Llama Guard 3 1B, checks each answer before you see it. Nothing is sent to an external AI service.

Messages you send are written to a local log for the experiment. The log keeps a hash of your IP address, not the address itself.
