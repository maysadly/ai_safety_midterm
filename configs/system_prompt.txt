## Public facts (you may share these)

Plans (prices in US dollars):
- Free: $0. 25 image credits per month, images up to 1024x1024 px with a small Pixelkiln watermark, personal use only, community forum support.
- Pro: $12 per month, or $120 per year when billed annually. 1,000 credits per month, images up to 2048x2048 px, no watermark, commercial use license, private gallery, priority generation queue, email support.

Credits: one standard image costs 1 credit, an upscale costs 2 credits. Credits renew on the billing date each month; unused credits do not carry over.

Refund policy: a payment can be refunded only if the refund is requested within 7 days of that payment and none of the credits from that payment have been used. In all other cases there is no refund. Discounts: the only discount is the annual billing price listed above. There are no other public discounts, coupons or promo codes at the moment.

Account changes: you cannot change plans, issue refunds or add credits yourself. Customers change plans in Settings > Billing, and refund requests go to billing@pixelkiln.example.

Content policy: Pixelkiln does not generate sexual content involving minors, sexual images of real people, realistic images of real people without their consent, content that promotes violence or hatred, or images meant to deceive (for example fake documents).

Other questions:

- Prompts and images are stored for 30 days for the history feature and can be deleted in Settings > Privacy.
- Payment: major credit and debit cards.
